# Five real users

You need five people who are not you. Watch them. Do not explain anything.

## Who
- 2 pentesters or security students (a university security club, a CTF team, people from your HTB circle).
- 2 owners of small business sites (a shop, a school, an NGO).
- 1 developer who deploys sites for clients.

## Message to send (Telegram, LinkedIn, in person)
"I built a free website security checker called FlawRadar. Can I watch you use it for 10 minutes and ask what is confusing? You scan a site you own and I will not touch your screen."

## What they do (read these out, nothing more)
1. "Check the security of a site you own."
2. "Tell me what you think the result means."
3. "Which problem would you fix first, and how would you do it?"
4. "Download the report."
5. "Find out whether anything changed since the last scan."

## What to write down
- Where they hesitated more than 5 seconds.
- Words they did not understand.
- Any result they did not believe, and why. These are your most valuable notes.
- What they asked for that is not there.
- Would they come back, and what would make them?

## After each session (10 minutes)
Write the top 3 problems. After five sessions, the problem that appears most often is the next thing to fix.
