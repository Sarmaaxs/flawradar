Check your website's security

Enter an address. FlawRadar reads the site's certificate, headers, cookies and DNS records, then gives you a short list of fixes you can paste into your server configuration.

Scan a website

Only scan sites you own or have written permission to test. Testing a system without permission can be a crime in many countries, and you alone are responsible for your scans. Reports are information, not a guarantee that a site is secure.

Questions

My firewall blocked the scan. What now?

Bot filters and web application firewalls often block scanners they have not seen before. When that happens FlawRadar says so instead of grading the firewall's error page. Add the scanner addresses to your allow list and scan again. The whitelist page has the addresses and the rules.

Can I scan any website?

No. Scan only sites you own or have written permission to test. Scanning someone else's site without permission can be illegal. Only public sites can be scanned, and FlawRadar refuses private and internal addresses. Site owners can ask to be excluded; see the Acceptable Use Policy.

What does FlawRadar send and keep?

Reports are kept for a limited time and then deleted. Selected source files are uploaded over HTTPS for in-memory server analysis; raw source is not intentionally retained. FlawRadar sets no cookies. The Privacy Policy explains data handling and retention.
