How it works

  1. You prove you control the site with a small file or a DNS record. This stops anyone from having a site scanned daily that they do not run.
  2. You enter the site and your email address here. A confirmation link is sent to that address.
  3. Once confirmed, the first scan sets the baseline. After that, a scan runs about every 24 hours.
  4. You get an email when the grade drops, or the score falls by 10 points or more. Nothing is sent when things stay the same.
  5. Every email has a link that stops the watch. Taking the proof off your site also stops it.

The scans are the normal read-only scan, from the published addresses. Your email address is used only for the confirmation and the alerts, and is deleted when you stop the watch. See the Privacy Policy.
