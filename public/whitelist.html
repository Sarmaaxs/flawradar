What the scanner does

  • Normal scan: about eight requests. The home page over HTTPS, one request to port 80, one CORS test, one or two requests for security.txt, a TLS handshake, and some DNS lookups.
  • Deep scan: about twenty more requests for well-known paths, spaced a fraction of a second apart. Deep scans only run against hosts whose owner has proved control.
  • Only GET, HEAD and one OPTIONS request. Nothing is submitted, no forms are filled in, nothing is guessed at, no passwords are tried.
  • No more than six scans of one host per hour, and twelve per hour from one visitor.
  • Responses are checked and discarded. The report keeps the findings, a few header values and short excerpts used as evidence.
